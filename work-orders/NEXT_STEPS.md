## What you bring back to me

When the Builder finishes a piece, it hands you a bundle of files. **Bring me that whole bundle, exactly as it gives it, without changing anything.**

I then check it two ways — I read it, and I actually run its tests myself in my own machine — and I tell you either:
- **"Good. Next."** or
- **"Here's a small fix"** (I'll give you one file to hand back to the Builder).

---

## The repeating loop

1. Builder finishes a piece.
2. You bring me its bundle.
3. I check it and re-run it.
4. I say "good" or "here's a fix."
5. If a fix: you give it to the Builder, it redoes the piece, back to step 2.
6. If good: on to the next piece.

Repeat until all 7 pieces + the "put it together" jobs are done. Then the Builder produces **one Android app file** — and you install it on your phone and start using it.
